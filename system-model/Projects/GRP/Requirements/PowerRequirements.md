# PowerRequirements

### Standard Mains Power Operation

The system shall operate from a standard single-phase UK/EU mains supply
(230 V AC, 50 Hz, steady-state rated current ≤ 5 A, inrush current ≤ 15 A for
≤ 20 ms) without requiring dedicated circuits, three-phase power, or
additional electrical infrastructure.
